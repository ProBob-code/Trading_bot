Cockpit Vision & Mechanics

The Cockpit is GoatBot V2's command center. It acts as the tactical execution sandbox where simulated parameters, indicators, and historical logs are aggregated into one single, transparent risk workspace.

Positions Tab

Tracks open trades with real-time unrealized P&L calculations. You can instantly manage stop losses or execute a panic liquidation via the close buttons.

History Tab

An immutable ledger displaying all past trades. It acts as an audit trail to measure closed profits, entry/exit efficiency, and platform statistics.

Signals Tab

Real-time feed showing dynamic Buy/Sell indicators generated by active quantitative engines (Neural Networks, Bollinger, and Ichimoku).

Portfolio Tab

Live risk analytics showing your win-rate, total closed metrics, profit factor, Sharpe ratios, and drawdowns to optimize capital sizing.
